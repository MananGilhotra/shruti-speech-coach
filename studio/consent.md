# Flaw Studio — volunteer information and consent (version 1.0)

**What this is.** SHRUTI is a student research prototype that gives feedback on speech delivery (pace, pauses, pitch, loudness, fluency, clarity, emphasis). To test it fairly we need real people reading short passages from famous public-domain speeches, sometimes well and sometimes with a deliberate delivery mistake that the app asks you to make.

**What you will do (about 15 minutes).**
1. Listen once to a 30–50 second excerpt of a historic speech.
2. Record yourself reading it as well as you can.
3. Record it three more times, each time making one mistake the app shows you (for example "rush the highlighted words" or "add an 'umm' before this word").
4. Repeat for a second excerpt.

**What we keep.** Your recordings, the instruction shown for each, and the optional details you choose to give (first language, accent region, gender, device, age band). We do **not** collect your name, email or phone number; you are identified only by a pseudonymous ID such as `spk014`.

**How it will be used and shared.** Recordings and labels will be published as part of the SHRUTI-Bench dataset under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)** licence, on GitHub / Hugging Face / Zenodo, for research and education on speech-delivery feedback. Anyone will be able to download and reuse them under that licence.

**Your voice is personal data.** Even without your name, people who know you might recognise your voice. Please only take part if you are comfortable with that.

**Voluntary and withdrawable.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time. You can ask for your recordings to be deleted until the dataset is frozen on **12 October 2026** by contacting the team with your pseudonymous ID; after release, copies others have downloaded cannot be recalled.

**Safety.** Your recordings are never used to clone your voice. SHRUTI's "Ghost Coach" only ever modifies a speaker's own uploaded recording, in that speaker's session.

**Eligibility.** You must be 18 or older.

By ticking the boxes in the app you confirm that you are 18 or older, that you have read this page, and that you agree to the release of your recordings under CC BY 4.0.
